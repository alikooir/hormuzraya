Raya: the intelligence behind our products

Instead of building each data product from scratch, we built one market intelligence graph. Tejaros and MapMarketing are powered by Raya, and every improvement to Raya improves them all.

Market intelligence graph

Raya

Raya is the market intelligence graph we built. It draws the big picture of a market: who is in it, what role they play and who they trade with. Data arrives from many sources, from world trade records to the web, duplicate companies are merged, and every player is scored on real data.

TejarosMapMarketing
  • Data collection from many sources, continuously refreshed
  • Companies, roles and trade relationships
  • Entity resolution and name matching
  • Opportunity scoring and ranking
  • Market trend analysis and forecasting
  • Modular: each market is a vertical on the same graph
DESIGN PRINCIPLES

How Raya is built

Many sources, one truth

Data comes from many sources and is standardised and de-duplicated before use.

Scores from evidence

Every rank and score traces back to the real data behind it.

Compute only when needed

Expensive results are cached and recomputed only when the data changes: faster answers, lower cost.

Model-agnostic AI

AI providers are interchangeable, with the right model for Farsi and for English.

Farsi and English

Raya is designed for both languages from the start.

Modular

Each new market or use is a module on the same infrastructure.
