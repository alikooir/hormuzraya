Zebel: One dashboard and inbox for multi-channel selling

A seller who sells on online marketplaces, Instagram, their own website and in person manages every order, message and stock level in one place.

Visit zebelapp.ir
THE PROBLEM

Why Zebel

Small shops now sell in several places at once. Customer messages are spread across several apps, stock levels on each channel drift apart, and an item that has sold out is still being sold somewhere else.

Orders, shipping and bookkeeping each happen separately, and the seller's time goes into switching between panels instead of selling.

Connects to Basalam, Instagram, WooCommerce, messengers and shipping services

FEATURES

What it does

Unified inbox

Messages from every sales network and messenger in one inbox.

Stock sync

A sale on any channel updates stock everywhere and prevents overselling.

Orders from every channel

Every order, with one consistent status, in one list.

Product publishing

Define a product once and publish it to several channels.

Shipping and in-person sales

Create shipments, track deliveries and sell in person by scanning items.

Wallet and bookkeeping

Revenue, fees and the shop's balance in one view.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Connect channels

    You connect your sales accounts to Zebel.

  2. Merge your catalogue

    Products and stock across channels are linked and kept in sync.

  3. Work in one place

    Messages and orders from every channel arrive in one dashboard.

  4. Ship and settle

    Shipments are booked and the books update automatically.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who it is for

  • Basalam sellers
  • Instagram shops
  • WooCommerce stores
  • Shops selling both in person and online
FAQ

Common questions

Which channels does Zebel connect to?

Basalam, Instagram, WooCommerce stores, messengers and shipping services. New channels are added over time.

Do I need technical skills to use Zebel?

No. Connecting channels and day-to-day work are designed for ordinary sellers.

Do in-person sales update stock too?

Yes. An in-person sale is recorded like any other channel and updates stock everywhere.
