MapMarketing: Sales leads from the map, paid per lead

Choose a business category and an area and receive the businesses there with contact details. You see the cost before you buy.

THE PROBLEM

Why MapMarketing

Anyone who sells to businesses, from distributors to software companies, needs an up-to-date list of prospects in each category and area.

Collecting it by hand from maps takes days, and ready-made lists are usually out of date and full of duplicates.

FEATURES

What it does

Precise search

Search by category, city and area on the map.

Cost before you buy

See the number of results and the cost before paying.

One price per lead

A flat price per lead, whatever its source.

No duplicates

Duplicates across sources are removed, so you never pay twice for one business.

Spreadsheet export and wallet

Download your results and pay from a prepaid wallet.

REST API

Connect directly to your CRM and sales tools.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Choose category and area

    For example, pharmacies in one district or a whole city.

  2. See the cost

    The result count and cost are shown before you buy.

  3. Pay from your wallet

    You pay only for the leads you receive.

  4. Download and use

    Download the list or send it to your system through the API.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who it is for

  • Distributors and wholesalers
  • B2B sales teams
  • Marketing agencies
  • Companies that serve businesses
PLATFORM

Built on Map data extractor

Local business data engine

Map data extractor

Our in-house engine collects data on businesses listed in map apps from several sources, removes duplicates and serves it to our products through an API.

  • Search by business category and area
  • Many sources, one consistent output
  • Cross-source de-duplication
  • Gauging each business's presence on the map

FAQ

Common questions

Where does the data come from?

From businesses' public information on map apps, collected, standardised and de-duplicated by our in-house engine.

How do I pay?

You top up your wallet and each lead you receive is charged from it. No monthly subscription.

Can I connect it to my CRM?

Yes. MapMarketing has a documented REST API.
