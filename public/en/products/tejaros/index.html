Tejaros: Trade intelligence and B2B lead generation

Finds potential buyers and importers in your target markets, evaluates and ranks them, and gives a clear view of the market, competitors and trends so export decisions rest on data.

THE PROBLEM

Why Tejaros

Manufacturers and traders mostly rely on intermediaries, trade fairs, personal networks and manual searching to find foreign customers. Information on markets, buyers and competitors sits in many scattered sources, often in other languages.

The result is unused export capacity, and a large share of each deal's margin going to a chain of intermediaries.

First focus: petroleum products, bitumen, sulphur, petrochemicals and minerals

FEATURES

What it does

Buyer discovery

Finds buyers, importers and distributors of your product in each country.

Company profiles and links

Each company is shown with its role, activity and trading partners.

Opportunity scoring

Buyers are scored and ranked on fit with your product and real evidence.

Lead management

Follow leads from first contact to negotiation in one panel.

Market analysis and forecasts

Dashboards for trade flows, prices and trend forecasts in target markets.

Enterprise panel

Multi-user panel with custom reports for holdings and trade bodies.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Pick product and markets

    You choose the product and target countries or regions.

  2. Market mapping

    Tejaros maps that market's players from many sources on the Raya graph.

  3. Evaluate and rank

    Potential buyers are evaluated and sorted by fit.

  4. Act

    You get a ranked list with profiles and analysis and follow it up in the panel.

About our platform

FAQ

Common questions

Is Tejaros just a database?

No. Tejaros collects and merges information from many sources, evaluates companies and their relationships, and returns a ranked buyer list with market analysis.

Which industries does it cover?

We started with petroleum products, bitumen, sulphur, petrochemicals and minerals, and are extending to food, agriculture, metals and other export goods.

Can you also do the selling for us?

Yes. With our export market development service our team finds the buyer, runs the negotiation and is paid a commission only on successful deals.
