Map Services Desk: Finding and fixing businesses' problems on map apps

With an agency that specialises in map apps, we find businesses with map problems and solve them, from listing to correction and ongoing support.

THE PROBLEM

Why Map Services Desk

Most customers now find businesses on the map. If the address, phone number or opening hours are wrong, there are no photos, or the business is not listed at all, the customer goes to a competitor.

Many business owners have no idea what their map listing looks like.

In partnership with an agency that specialises in map apps

FEATURES

What it does

Map presence check

We check how the business appears on the widely used map apps.

Listing

We list businesses that are not on the map yet.

Corrections

We correct the address, phone, hours and category.

Profile completion

We add photos, a description and full details.

Support and monitoring

We watch for changes and support you afterwards.

Internal desk

Every step from discovery to sale and delivery is tracked in our desk.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Discover

    Our engine finds businesses with map problems.

  2. Tell the owner

    The owner is shown the problem and the fix.

  3. Fix

    Our partner agency does the listing and corrections.

  4. Support

    The listing is monitored and support continues.
