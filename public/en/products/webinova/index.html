Webinova: an AI site builder that makes a complete business website from a few photos and a few lines

Pick your business type, upload a few photos and describe your business in a few lines. AI builds a complete, ready-to-publish website in Farsi or English.

THE PROBLEM

Why Webinova

Many small businesses still have no website. Custom design is expensive and slow, ordinary site builders take time and skill, and writing website copy is hard for most owners.

Model-agnostic: several AI providers with automatic fallback

AI-written copy

Farsi and English

Build the site in either language.

Preview and edit

FAQ

Common questions

No. You give your trade, a description and photos, and Webinova does the rest.

Yes. Every part can be edited before and after publishing, or regenerated with one click.
