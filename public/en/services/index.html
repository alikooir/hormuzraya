Services we provide

The engines, data and team behind our products go to work on your business problem.

Core service

Custom intelligent solutions

We solve problems no ready-made tool covers, with data and AI.

Every business has a problem no ready-made tool solves: finding customers in a particular market, automating a manual process, bringing scattered data together, or deciding with incomplete information. We define the problem with you, find the data it needs, and build and deploy a solution based on AI and data.

What you get

  • A clear problem statement and success measure
  • Collection and cleaning of the data it needs
  • A solution built and deployed on our infrastructure or yours
  • Measured results and ongoing improvement

Export market development

We find the buyer and close the deal; commission on successful deals only.

Here we do more than sell software. Using data and analysis we find the target market and the right buyers, make first contact, propose how to approach and negotiate, and our specialists carry the deal through to completion. The exporter pays nothing until a deal succeeds, and the commission follows that market's usual rate.

What you get

  • Target market identification
  • Buyer discovery and evaluation
  • First contact and negotiation
  • Follow-through to a closed deal

Related product: Tejaros

Market research and reports

Custom reports on a market's players, trade flows, prices and openings.

For a given product, market or region we prepare a custom report: the main players and their relationships, trade flows, prices, competitors and openings. Reports can be one-off or recurring, and can include competitor monitoring and opportunity alerts.

What you get

  • A map of market players
  • Trade flow and price analysis
  • Competitor monitoring
  • Alerts on new opportunities

Related product: Tejaros

Enterprise panels

Dedicated multi-user panels for holdings and trade bodies.

For holdings, chambers of commerce, trade unions, trade promotion bodies and large companies we set up a dedicated panel with many users, a management dashboard and custom reports.

What you get

  • A panel shaped to your organisation
  • Many users and access levels
  • Management dashboard
  • Custom analytical reports

Related product: Tejaros

Data and API

Programmatic access to business data and sales leads.

We deliver business data and sales leads to your systems through a documented API, straight into your CRM, sales automation or internal analytics.

What you get

  • A documented REST API
  • Consistent, de-duplicated data
  • Pay for what you use

Related product: LocationMarketing

Map presence

Listing, correcting and completing your business on map apps.

Together with an agency that specialises in map apps, we check, list, correct and complete your business on map apps so customers find you easily and trust what they see. Every step, from spotting businesses with map problems to delivery and support, runs in a desk we built for this partnership, powered by Raya.

What you get

  • A check of your current listing
  • Listing or corrections
  • Photos and a full description
  • Support afterwards

Multi-channel selling

Setting up your shop's channels and connecting them to one panel.

We set up your shop's online and in-person sales channels and connect them to a single panel, so orders, messages and stock are managed in one place.

What you get

  • Channel connection
  • Catalogue and stock merge
  • Training on the panel

Related product: Zebel
